# OLIPOP — Brand DNA

Source: drinkolipop.com (homepage, /pages/ingredients, /pages/our-story, /pages/faq). Verify against live site before ad launch — flavors/claims change.

## What it is
"The feel good soda" — healthy prebiotic soda. Real ingredients, classic soda flavor, functional fiber/prebiotic blend (OLISMART®). Founded by Ben and David. Mission: "Feed Life" — nourish the microbiome, prove health and indulgence aren't mutually exclusive.

- **9g Fiber** (refrigerated): Blackberry Vanilla, Crisp Apple, Classic Grape, Classic Root Beer, Vintage Cola, Cream Soda, Strawberry Vanilla, Cherry Cola, Orange Cream, Peaches & Cream, Orange Squeeze, Ginger Ale, Citrus Rush, Doctor Goodwin, Cherry Vanilla, Tropical Punch, Lemon Lime, Ginger Lemon, Watermelon Lime, Banana Cream
- **6g Fiber** (shelf-stable): Raspberry Sherbet, Shirley Temple, Pineapple Paradise (limited), select 9g flavors
- Variety packs: Classic Soda, Fruity Fun, Favorites
- Caffeinated flavors (call out clearly, never bury): Vintage Cola, Doctor Goodwin, Cherry Cola (50mg), Citrus Rush (60mg). All others caffeine-free.

## Ingredients (say exactly this, don't embellish)
- Prebiotic fiber: cassava root (resistant dextrin), chicory root (inulin), Jerusalem artichoke (inulin), acacia fiber, guar fiber
- Botanicals: nopal cactus, calendula flower, kudzu root, marshmallow root
- Sweeteners: cassava syrup, fruit juice, stevia
- Non-GMO verified, vegan, gluten-free, paleo-compliant

## Nutrition facts (use exact numbers only)
- 2–5g sugar per can (claim "80% less sugar than traditional soda" — comparative, sourced)
- 35–45 calories per can
- 9g or 6g fiber per can depending on line
- "Excellent Source of Fiber" per FDA labeling standard — this is an FDA *labeling* threshold, not FDA endorsement. Never say "FDA approved."

- "Purdue University research on OLIPOP's prebiotic fiber blend showed measurable gut health impact" (cite the study, don't overstate as "clinically proven to cure/fix")
- Taste/sensory descriptors: "gently sweet," "tastes like classic soda"

## NEVER say in Meta ads (compliance-critical)
- **No disease claims.** Never say OLIPOP treats, cures, prevents, or reverses any disease/condition (IBS, bloating disorders, obesity, diabetes, etc.). "Supports digestive health" is fine; "fixes your gut" or "cures bloating" is not.
- **No "probiotic."** It is PREBIOTIC (fiber that feeds gut bacteria), not probiotic (live bacteria). Never conflate — factually wrong and a compliance risk.
- **No "FDA approved."** FDA doesn't approve foods/beverages this way. Only reference the "Excellent Source of Fiber" labeling threshold, worded exactly like that.
- **No diabetic-safe / weight-loss guarantees.** Site explicitly says "consult your healthcare provider" for diabetics and before adding to a child's or pregnant/breastfeeding person's diet — never state or imply it's medically cleared for these groups without that disclaimer.
- **No personal attribute targeting.** Never imply the viewer specifically has a condition ("you have IBS," "you're bloated," "you're pre-diabetic") — Meta policy violation regardless of truth.
- **No before/after body imagery or weight-loss framing.** This is a soda, not a diet product.
- **No unqualified "healthy" as a standalone absolute claim without support** — always anchor to a specific fact (low sugar, fiber content) rather than a bare "healthy" assertion.
- **No omitting caffeine** on caffeinated flavors (Vintage Cola, Doctor Goodwin, Cherry Cola, Citrus Rush).
- **No hiding the allergen note**: produced in a facility that also processes tree nuts/peanuts (product itself contains neither).
- **No superiority claims against named competitor brands** without substantiation on hand.

## Visual identity
- Playful, retro-soda-fountain aesthetic reinterpreted with modern/clean packaging — pastel-forward but vivid can colors, one hero color per flavor.
- Photography: real cans, bright/clean backgrounds, condensation/refreshment cues, ingredient callouts (root/botanical imagery) alongside cans.
- Tone of visuals: fun and nostalgic (classic soda names — root beer, cola, cream soda) paired with "better-for-you" cues (fiber content badges, ingredient icons).
- Wordmark: OLIPOP, often paired with tagline "the feel good soda."
