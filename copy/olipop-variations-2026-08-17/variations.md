## Top 3 picks for testing first

1. **Variation 7 (Billboard, contrarian, direct CTA)** — broadest cold-reach format, six-word claim is the fastest read on the platform, cheapest to prove/disprove the core hook before spending on longer-copy variants.
2. **Variation 2 (Testimonial, social proof, sourced quote)** — real voice-of-customer language is the strongest trust signal available and this is the shortest, cheapest-to-produce format to test it in.
3. **Variation 4 (Benefit stack, gut-health seeker)** — targets the persona most likely to convert on function over nostalgia, and benefit-stack format tends to perform well for cold-traffic education without needing the length of problem–solution.

Run these three first; let performance data pick the format/persona combination before spending budget on the remaining nine.
